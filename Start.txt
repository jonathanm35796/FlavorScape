# Lletya — Source Selection and Development Strategy

I want to revive my former RuneScape Private Server, Lletya.

My inspirations are Runite for authentic, responsive PvP combat and OS-PvP for its polished standalone client with custom settings.

**I strongly prefer adapting a reputable, publicly available RSPS source over building an entire game from scratch.**

I am willing to replace or substantially rewrite individual components where necessary, but I do not want to recreate mature systems unnecessarily.

## Development priorities

1. Find a reputable, well-developed OSRS-style server source with a compatible standalone client.
2. Prioritize accurate combat, responsive PKing, reliable networking, and correct game-tick processing.
3. Favor an existing source with substantial working gameplay and content over a bare framework, provided its architecture is maintainable.
4. Develop a dedicated Lletya client with configurable settings, custom interfaces, hotkeys, performance options, and PvP quality-of-life features.
5. Do not require RuneLite compatibility.
6. Preserve working systems and improve them incrementally.
7. Avoid outdated, insecure, incomplete, or poorly structured sources.
8. Evaluate licensing, redistribution permissions, and intellectual-property risks before adopting a source.

## Initial candidates

Research and compare:

- RS Mod: https://github.com/rsmod/rsmod
- RSBox: https://github.com/vanicosrs/rsbox
- Tarnish: https://rsps.org/resources/tarnish

Expand the search to Rune-Server.org and other credible RSPS development communities.

Do not limit the evaluation to these three candidates.

## Instructions for Codex

Act as an experienced RSPS developer and Java game-server engineer.

Before writing code, conduct a detailed source-selection investigation.

For each promising candidate:

- Inspect the actual repository and code.
- Verify that it builds.
- Determine whether a compatible standalone client is available.
- Assess existing PvP functionality and combat accuracy.
- Evaluate the completeness of gameplay systems.
- Identify bugs, security concerns, and technical debt.
- Assess the difficulty of implementing custom client settings.
- Review licensing and project maintenance.

Distinguish verified findings from assumptions. Do not equate popularity with quality.

Recommend the strongest foundation for a long-term Lletya project.

**Do not start from scratch unless the evidence demonstrates that adapting existing sources would be more difficult or less maintainable.**

The first development milestone should be a working local Lletya client and server, followed by improvements to PvP combat and client customization.

Do not begin major implementation until I approve the recommended source.
