# Edgeville workshop and PvP market

Shop update reverted 2026-10-27: removed the supplemental 1,311-item supply catalogue and Skilling Market option. The original General Market / PvP Equipment dropdown, catalogue eligibility, prices and 40% buyback are restored. The earlier interface, menu, payment and closing fixes remain. Restart client and server to apply the reversion.

Edgeville bank retains its original layout, bankers, Grand Exchange clerks and security advisor. The workshop changes occupy the existing room within `(3091,3506,0)` to `(3098,3511,0)`. The existing FlavorScape rejuvenation pool moves from `(3097,3508,0)` to `(3097,3510,0)`; its two-by-two footprint ends at the northeast corner `(3098,3511,0)`. A counter at `(3097,3506,0)` separates the public approach at `(3096,3506,0)` from the **PvP shop** merchant at `(3098,3506,0)`. The merchant uses Shanomi's appearance with a Trade action. The room's existing altar and chest remain.

The market uses the arrangement in `Desired Market.png`, with a simpler Old School brown panel, native cached close/scroll-arrow sprites, a centered bold market title and bold category headers. It includes a market selector, nine category buttons, search, scrolling item icons with prices and a close button. Click the selector again, click outside its menu or press Escape to dismiss it; Escape dismisses the menu before closing the shop. All categories appear in one continuous scroll document, each separated by a named header. Clicking a category scrolls to its section while respecting the bottom of the document. The sidebar marks the chosen section, updating as the player scrolls manually. The scrollbar uses the native client's palette and geometry: small arrow steps, page-sized track clicks and thumb dragging that preserves the grab position. Scrolling stops at the end of the catalogue without blank trailing pages. Search and the PvP Equipment selector filter the catalogue while retaining section headers. Left-click reports the server's per-item price in general chat without buying. Right-click uses the standard client context-menu presentation with **Buy 1**, **Buy 5**, **Buy 10** and **Buy X**; Buy X opens the game's numeric amount dialogue. Menu hover text names the actual catalogue item under the cursor. The former custom-painted context menu, quantity buttons, Buy 50 and Buy All actions have been removed. Search accepts a name and Enter. Sell from the inventory's normal shop menu. The displayed balance includes Blood Money and platinum tokens, valued at 1,000 Blood Money per token, and abbreviates large totals with K/M/B suffixes (for example, 3,000,000 tokens displays as 3B). Numeric submissions recheck the active shop, distance, combat/lock state, currency and inventory capacity. `Current market.png` and `Overlay.png` record the superseded appearance.

The General Market consolidates eligible items from existing Blood Money shops, retaining the lowest existing retail price when shops overlap. Item requirements and additional-payment purchases are excluded rather than bypassed. Freely spawnable items and currencies are excluded. The PvP Equipment selector filters the same catalogue to Ancient Warriors' gear. Ancient Warriors' items not previously sold by cash shops use their configured protection values as retail prices.

Buyback is `floor(retail price × 40 / 100)` Blood Money per item, including eligible noted and used items. Only catalogue items can be sold; selling never introduces new stock. Buyback works across categories. Items whose rounded buyback is zero cannot be sold. Transactions check the active shop, distance, combat state, inventory space, available currency and integer limits. Client requests never supply prices.

The native shop remains underneath as a fallback if the Market plugin is disabled. The server sends the complete custom catalogue before opening the native shop widgets, so the fallback window cannot appear while the catalogue loads. Previous shop interfaces close before publishing the new catalogue, ensuring an old CLOSE acknowledgement cannot clear the new shop data. Closing the custom panel detaches both native shop windows on the client thread before removing the overlay, without waiting for the server acknowledgement. The matching server close request runs in the background; late catalogue updates for that dismissed session are ignored. While the native right-click menu is open, clicks pass through the custom panel so choosing an option executes it and dismisses the menu. The custom client panel receives its catalogue and prices from the server. No bank floor or collision overrides are applied.
